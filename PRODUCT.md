# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Business owners and teams looking for an experienced, practical partner to design and build websites, mobile apps, and custom software. They should quickly understand what Bootpack Digital does, feel confident in the team's ability, and find a clear path to learn more or start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Bootpack Digital's website presents the agency's capabilities, work, and point of view. It should turn uncertainty into confidence by showing that the team builds reliable digital products, collaborates like a real partner, and can help clients reach their next stage.

## Brand Personality

Approachable, capable, and adventurous. The voice is friendly and direct, with an outdoorsy Utah character that feels natural to the Bootpack story. Personality should support clarity rather than compete with it.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS styling, stiff corporate formality, and overly cute or gimmicky humor. Do not imitate an unrelated reference brand; the existing Bootpack Digital site and its skiing and navigation story are the primary reference.

## Design Principles

- Make the next step obvious and useful.
- Show capability through clarity, craft, and real work.
- Use the outdoors story as authentic character, not decoration for its own sake.
- Sound like an experienced human partner, not an agency template.
- Keep playful moments grounded and concise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient color contrast, semantic structure, useful alternative text, and reduced-motion preferences.
