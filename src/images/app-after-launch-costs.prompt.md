## Prompt

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: editorial cover illustration for Bootpack Digital blog post "What does a custom app cost after launch?"
Create one polished landscape illustration, 3:2 ratio. A thoughtfully arranged paper workbench showing a finished web app as a small clean browser window in the center. Around it, four tangible, clearly distinct layers of ongoing operation: a modest server/database stack, a small shield and maintenance tool, a notification envelope, and a neatly organized ledger with a simple unlabelled bar chart. The objects should feel like parts of one operating product, connected through subtle ink lines on the paper. No currency symbols, no readable numbers or text. Tactile cut-paper editorial illustration, fine ink outlines, subtle paper grain, controlled shadows, confident human composition with breathing room. Palette: deep navy #001c2b, muted blue #2787b7, ice blue #e9f3f8, restrained coral #f06052, warm white. Main subject clear at blog-card size and central 1.91:1 crop. Avoid people, robots, brains, glowing circuitry, icon confetti, brand logos, text, watermarks, gradients, photorealism.
